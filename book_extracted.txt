Problem Overview and Background - ADS MAKER Project General Background In today's digital marketing landscape, businesses rely on a wide variety of independent and disconnected tools to create and manage advertising content. These tools include visual design platforms like Canva, AI-based text generators like ChatGPT, and paid advertising platforms like Google Ads and Facebook Ads. However,  the process of creating a single effective digital advertisement  requires constant switching between multiple unconnected tools, creating significant challenges in the workflow. The Core Problem: Fragmentation and Lack of Integration Currently,  there is no comprehensive solution  that unifies all stages of advertisement creation into a single, intuitive interface. The ad creation process requires working with multiple different platforms, each providing only partial functionality, leading to three main problems: Lack of Brand Consistency  - When work is performed across multiple separate tools, it is difficult to maintain uniformity and consistency in messaging, colors, and visual style of the brand Loss of Efficiency  - Significant time is wasted in transitions between different platforms, copying content, and synchronizing between systems that do not communicate with each other Limited Personalization  - Difficulty in creating fully customized advertisements for different target audiences when the process is fragmented across different tools Gaps in Current Tools Analysis of the leading tools in the market reveals the following limitations: Canva Simplifies the graphic design process But lacks  direct integration with automated video creation or copywriting tools Requires manual work in combining text and design Adobe Creative Suite Provides high-level professional tools But requires  advanced graphic design skills Demands significant manual effort and time investment ChatGPT and Similar AI Text Tools Can generate compelling and high-quality advertising content But are not naturally connected  to design or publishing platforms Require manual copying of content to other tools Google Ads and Facebook Ads Offer powerful and advanced distribution networks But the creative process  (text and design) must occur externally No built-in tools for content creation or design The Critical Challenge This fragmentation highlights a  critical challenge in modern advertising : the absence of an integrated, automated platform that connects  strategy, creativity, and execution  under one roof. Businesses are forced to invest significant resources in coordinating between different tools, learning multiple platforms, and managing a fragmented and inefficient workflow. Lack of Hebrew Language Support Beyond the technological fragmentation, there is  another significant issue : most existing tools do not provide full and high-quality support for the Hebrew language and RTL (Right-to-Left) direction. This particularly challenges businesses in the Israeli market that need high-quality advertisements in Hebrew, requiring manual adjustments and numerous corrections. The Need for a Comprehensive Solution This situation creates a  significant need  for a single platform that will enable: Campaign data input in one place Automatic textual content creation Automatic and customized graphic design Seamless integration with advertising platforms Full support for Hebrew and RTL Maintaining brand consistency Campaign tracking and management from one location Review of Existing Tools - Preliminary Research As part of the project preparation, a  comprehensive market research  was conducted, including a review of popular tools used for content creation, ad design, and campaign distribution. The purpose of the research was to identify the  key criteria  for comparing existing tools:     API Availability  - Whether the tool provides an external API for integration    Hebrew Language Support  - Level of support for Hebrew and RTL     Pricing Models  - Costs and economic accessibility    Integration Ease  - Complexity of incorporation into a unified system    Automation Capabilities  - Level of possible automation The tools were divided into  three main categories  corresponding to the different stages of ad creation: Content Creation Tools  - For creating marketing text, headlines, and descriptions Ad Generation Tools  - For visual design and combining content with graphics Marketing & Distribution Tools  - For publishing and managing campaigns Example from Content Creation Category: Writer.com As part of the research, tools like  Writer.com  were examined - a professional AI-powered writing platform built for enterprise-level content creation. The platform is powered by its proprietary language model,  Palmyra , and specializes in producing brand-consistent marketing content including marketing emails, blog posts, technical documentation, and promotional content. Advantages: Support for brand-consistent content Integration with CRM and CMS systems Available API for large-scale automation Limitations: Requires payment for most advanced capabilities Not naturally connected to design tools Requires manual copying to other platforms Problem Summary The analysis reveals that despite the existence of advanced and powerful tools in each domain (content creation, design, and distribution),  there is currently no single solution that integrates all stages  into one smooth, automated, and unified process. This fragmentation causes time waste, lack of consistency, high costs, and limits the ability of small and medium-sized businesses to produce high-quality advertisements without technical expertise or large budgets. 2. Development Process Description 3.1 Development Methodology The development of the Ads-Maker system followed an  Agile Development  approach with short two-week iterations. The choice of this methodology stemmed from the need for flexibility and the ability to respond quickly to findings discovered during development. The process was divided into two main phases:  Phase A  focused on planning and research, and  Phase B  focused on practical implementation and deployment. 3.2 Phase A - Planning and Research  At the project's outset, comprehensive market research was conducted, including a systematic review of leading platforms in content creation and advertising (Canva, ChatGPT, Adobe Creative Suite). Concurrently,  structured interviews were held with 15 small business owners  from various sectors to identify pain points in the advertisement creation process. The findings revealed three core needs:  simplicity and speed ,  reasonable cost , and  quality support for Hebrew and Arabic . Based on the research findings, the system's hierarchical structure was designed (Company → Campaign → Agent → Advertisement) along with a MongoDB database. At this stage, initial technologies were selected: React.js for the user interface, Node.js for the server, ChatGPT API for content generation, and Canva API for graphic design. Subsequently, a  Proof of Concept (POC)  was developed to test technical feasibility, which revealed limitations in Hebrew content quality and high costs of the selected technologies - findings that led to significant changes in the implementation phase. 3.3 Phase B - Practical Implementation, Integrations, and Deployment  Backend Development: Development of the central API server began using Node.js and Express. The server includes endpoints for all system operations: creating and managing companies, campaigns, agents, and advertisements.  Authentication  (user verification) and  Authorization  (access permissions) mechanisms were implemented to ensure data security. Every request is validated for data integrity before processing. At this stage, after thorough testing, a  strategic decision was made to change the AI engine : transitioning from ChatGPT to  Google Gemini API (gemini-2.5-flash) . The decision was based on three key considerations: Hebrew Content Quality  - A/B comparative testing showed approximately 30% improvement in Hebrew text quality generated by Gemini, with fewer grammatical errors and more natural marketing terminology. Cost  - Gemini API calls cost approximately 70% less than ChatGPT, a critical consideration for the system's economic viability. Speed  - Gemini's average response time is approximately 40% faster, significantly improving user experience. The server was integrated with MongoDB Atlas (cloud service), and complex business logic was implemented for managing advertisement states (pending, approved, rejected), performance tracking, and sending notifications. Frontend Development: A complete user interface was developed in React.js with  support for three languages : Hebrew, Arabic, and English. Automatic language detection and dynamic switching between  RTL  (for Hebrew and Arabic) and  LTR  (for English) were implemented. All interface components were designed to be responsive and automatically adapt to different screen sizes (Desktop, Tablet, Mobile). At this stage,  another strategic decision was made to change the graphic design engine : transitioning from Canva API to  Pixi.js  (WebGL rendering library). The decision was based on: Complete Control  - Pixi.js allows full customization of design templates without dependency on an external provider, including building unique templates adapted to RTL requirements. Cost  - Pixi.js is an open-source library with no cost, while Canva API requires an expensive subscription. Annual savings are estimated at over $6,000. Technical Flexibility  - Pixi.js operates client-side without Rate Limiting restrictions, enabling unlimited advertisement creation at no additional cost. Development of Advanced Features: Beyond basic advertisement creation functionality, additional advanced features were developed: Approval/Rejection Mechanism  - Allows company managers to review and approve or reject advertisements created by agents, while providing detailed feedback and improvement notes. Email Service  - An automated notification system based on SMTP, sending emails to agents when advertisements are rejected. The email includes the rejection reason and concrete improvement suggestions automatically generated by Gemini. Performance Monitoring Scheduler  - A background process that runs daily and checks active advertisement performance. The process analyzes performance metrics (CTR, Conversion Rate, ROI), and in case of low performance, sends an alert to the campaign manager and suggests advertisement alternatives. Comprehensive Testing: Testing was conducted at three levels: Unit Tests  - Testing each function individually to ensure it performs the expected operation. Integration Tests  - Testing communication and integration between different components: Frontend-Backend-Database-External APIs. End-to-End Tests  - Simulation of complete usage scenarios from start to finish, including creating a company, campaign, agent, and advertisement. Beta Testing with Real Users: Towards the end of development, the system was tested by  5 real businesses  for two weeks. Users reported bugs, usability issues, and response times. During this period, 23 bugs were identified and fixed, and user interface improvements were made based on feedback. 90% of participants rated the interface as "very easy to use," and average advertisement creation time was 52 seconds - meeting the goal of less than one minute. Deployment to Production Environment: After completing testing, the system was deployed to Production environment: Frontend  - Deployed on  Vercel , a cloud platform providing high performance, fast loading times, and 99.9% availability. Backend  - Deployed on  Render , a cloud platform providing Auto-scaling (automatic adjustment to user numbers), automatic backups, and performance monitoring. Iterative Development Process: Throughout the development process, an iterative approach was maintained: feature development, testing, feedback collection, improvement. This approach enabled early problem identification and resolution before they became critical. Additionally, it allowed strategic changes (such as transitioning to Gemini and Pixi.js) without affecting the overall timeline. 4. Below are the main challenges and the solutions that were developed.   5.1 Technical/Engineering Challenges Challenge 1: Full RTL Support for Hebrew and Arabic Problem Description: Most modern UI libraries do not fully support right-to-left writing direction. The Pixi.js graphics library, chosen for creating advertisements, did not include built-in RTL support. The problem manifested in incorrect text display, improper element alignment, and inconsistent behavior across different browsers. Implemented Solution: Custom wrapper functions were developed that automatically handle writing direction detection and adjust text display accordingly. The solution included integration of dedicated CSS properties for text direction along with adjustments at the design engine  level. Extensive testing was conducted across five different browsers to ensure proper display on all platforms. Result: Full RTL support was achieved with proper display across all browsers and devices. The system automatically detects the language and adjusts the display direction without requiring user intervention. Challenge 2: Integration with Google Gemini API Problem Description: In the early stages of development, content generated by Gemini API was not always of the required quality. Issues included grammatical errors in Hebrew, marketing terminology not common in the Israeli market, and text that sounded translated rather than natural. Implemented Solution: A customized and detailed Prompt was developed for each language, with clear instructions for the AI to create natural and high-quality marketing content. Multiple iterations of Prompt improvement were conducted based on feedback from Beta Testing users. Each version was tested for content quality and improved accordingly. Result: Significant improvement in generated content quality. User ratings increased from 3.8 to 4.8 out of 5, and the text became more natural and suitable for the Israeli market. 5.2 Analytical Challenges Challenge 3: Tracking and Measuring Advertisement Performance Problem Description: How to accurately measure the success of created advertisements? A reliable tracking mechanism was needed to enable businesses to understand which advertisements succeed and which need improvement. The problem included the need for a unique identification system for each advertisement and a mechanism for collecting actual performance data. Implemented Solution: QR Code Tracking System: Each advertisement created in the system received a unique QR Code automatically embedded in the advertisement design. The barcode includes a unique identifier that enables precise tracking of each advertisement. When a user scans the barcode, the system automatically updates the scan counter in the database and saves additional information such as scan date . Comprehensive Analytics Page: A dedicated Analytics page was developed displaying detailed statistics for each advertisement: QR scan count - how many people scanned the advertisement Click count - how many people clicked on links in the advertisement Visual graphs - graphical display of data over time Advertisement comparison - which advertisement performed better Performance data by campaign - centralized view of all campaign advertisements Data Collection Mechanism: Various communication interfaces were developed for collecting and processing data from barcode scans and advertisement clicks. The system processes and summarizes the data automatically and displays it in a convenient and clear interface. Result: A comprehensive and reliable tracking system that enables businesses to measure advertisement success in real-time. The collected data allows informed decisions about which advertisements to continue using and which need improvement. The system supports complete transparency and provides users with a clear picture of their advertisement performance. Summary: Dealing with these challenges required technical flexibility, creative thinking, and use of practical and applicable solutions. Each developed solution contributed to the system's success and achievement of the set goals.
